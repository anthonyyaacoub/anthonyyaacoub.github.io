Privacy policy

Apollo is a personal app used only by its owner. It reads the owner's own email and calendar with read-only access.

It stores email metadata (sender, subject, short snippet) on the owner's private computer. It never sends, deletes or shares mail, and no data is given to anyone else.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
